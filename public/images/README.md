## 🎨 How to Use Your Images

### Adding Images:
1. Copy your block printing photos to this folder
2. Follow the folder structure above
3. The website will automatically use them

### Image Specifications:
- **Hero images**: 1920x1080px or larger (16:9 ratio)
- **Product images**: 800x800px (square for collections)
- **Background images**: 1920x1080px or larger
- **Screen images**: 1920x1080px or larger
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Quality**: High quality (at least 72 DPI)

## ✅ Once You Add Images:

The website will automatically use them:
1. Hero section will display your photos
2. Product cards will show your actual products
3. Backgrounds will use your designs/motifs
4. Sections will use screen images as headers

## 🔗 File References in Code:

Images are referenced from this folder:
